## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Air University accredited?

Yes, Air University was accredited in 2004 and before 2004, Community College of the Air Force was accredited since 1980 by Southern Association of Colleges and Schools. Community College of the Air Force was moved under Air University in 2004.

### Can we accept American Council on Education (ACE) transcripts for evaluation?

Yes. ACE transcripts must be sent directly from [American Council on Education (ACE)](https://www.acenet.edu/programs-services/pages/credit-transcripts/request-transcripts.aspx). We do not accept ACE transcripts sent directly by StraighterLine or Sophia Learning.
