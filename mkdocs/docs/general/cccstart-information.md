# CCCStart Information

CCCStart is the new California Community Colleges starting experience for students. Santa Ana College is transitioning to CCCStart to help students begin their enrollment journey.

## Staff Guide

The CCCStart Staff Guide provides guidance for helping students with CCCStart, including identity verification, the application process, application review in Outcomes, and clearing fraud holds.
