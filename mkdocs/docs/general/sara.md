# State Authorization Reciprocity Agreements

## Overview

SARA (the State Authorization Reciprocity Agreement) is a voluntary agreement among participating states, territories, and districts. It provides eligible institutions located in a SARA member state a streamlined way to obtain authorization to offer distance education in other SARA member states.

California is the only state that has not joined SARA. Because Rancho Santiago Community College District is located in California, SAC cannot use SARA reciprocity to obtain authorization in other states. Instead, SAC must meet each state's individual authorization requirements or qualify for an exemption. This is why the states where SAC may serve students are limited by state-specific approvals and exemptions.

## Purpose

The primary purposes of SARA are to:

- Remove barriers to interstate distance education
- Ensure consumer protection for distance education students
- Provide a streamlined approval process for institutions offering courses online
- Maintain academic quality and accountability

## Key Points for Admissions Staff

- When processing applications from out-of-state students, verify their state of residence
- Check the state-specific status and any limitations listed above before advising students about online/distance education
- Louisiana students have a specific limitation: they cannot take courses with clinical or practicum requirements
- New Jersey and Oklahoma status is limited to situations with no physical presence requirement
- For students from states not listed above, or if the status or requirements are unclear, refer to your supervisor for an eligibility determination
