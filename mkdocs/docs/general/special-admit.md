# Special Admit

## Enrollment Fee Waiver for Special Admit Students

- Regular Special Admit part-time students enrolled in fewer than 12 units during the semester will have their Enrollment Fee waived.
- CCAP Special Admit part-time students from CCAP partner schools will have their Enrollment Fee waived when enrolled in 15 units or fewer, across no more than 4 courses, during the fall and spring semesters.
- Students who exceed the applicable unit (or course) limit for their admit type will be charged the current Enrollment Fee for all enrolled units, not just the units over the limit.
