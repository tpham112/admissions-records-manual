# Process Parchment Transcript Orders

## Processing Steps

1. Log into your **Parchment Exchange** account at [exchange.parchment.com](https://exchange.parchment.com/u/auth/login)

2. Click **Resume Printing**.

3. **Open Orders** tab should be opened by default. 

4. Hover mouse over each student and click **More**.

5. Under **Order Information**, check "Are you sending this transcript to a UC campus?"

    - If yes, navigate to **TRAN** on Colleague and run their transcript. Check and see if there is an IGETC Certification posted.
        - If there is, check on Laserfiche for certification.
        - If certification is missing, reach out to Graduation Office through Teams for the certification.
        - Once certification is available, download from Laserfiche.
        - Click on **Add Attachments** button for the desired student.
        - Click on **Add Another File**.
        - Drag and drop the certification into the **Upload New File** area.
        - Click **Save & Close**.
        - Navigate back to **Open Orders** main page and click **Process Order** for student.
    - If no, then simply click **Process Order** for student.

6. Click on **Problems** tab.

7. Click on each student name to view details.

8. Open **NAE** on Colleague and ensure **Name When Enrolled** matches **NAE** record.

    - If name does not match, click on **Edit** button next to **Name**.
        - Update **Name When Enrolled** to match **NAE**.
        - Click **Save Changes**.
        - Exit student details by clicking **X** button in upper-right hand corner.
        - Click **Resolve**.
        - Click **Process Order**.

    - If we cannot find the student in our records, click **Cancel Order**.
        - Select Cancel Reason.
        - Write comments.
        - Click **Yes, Cancel This Order**.

9. Go to the **History** tab.

10. Click on **Down Arrow** next to Delivery ID.

11. Update Date Range to yesterday's date to today's date.

12. Update Delivery Methods to Overnight.

13. Click Search.

14. Click on each student name.

15. Click Shipping Label to print out.

16. Navigate back to To Do page.

17. Click on In Process tab.

18. Make sure that there are no more envelope icons.

!!! danger "PAUSE!!!"
    Make sure there is transcript paper in the printer before next steps!!!

19. Go to the **STRQ screen** in Colleague to print paper transcripts to be mailed out.

20. Select today's date for Request Dates (start with the right field first).

21. Click **Save All** 2x.

22. Update settings.

23. Click **Save All**.

24. Fold and pack transcripts and drop off at Mailroom before 12pm.

6. Click **Pause Printing** on Parchment after paper transcripts are printed.

!!! warning "FEDEX"
    Automatic FedEx pick-up days are **Wednesday** — cancel or schedule accordingly.
