# Residency Student Scenarios & Additional Notes

[← California Residency processing guide](residency.md)

## High School and Enrollment Scenarios

### Eligible with documentation: high school graduate or equivalent but under 18

- Student must provide 2 proofs of CA residency from parent/guardian.
    - One proof must be a CA 540 showing the student as a dependent.

### High School Students

STPE → correct semester → Overload Petition Status → `NONRE` Non-Resident Waiver → Save and Update → Rebill.

### Incoming Freshmen

- If they marked something wrong: provide high school transcripts showing they've been in CA for at least the past year.

### State of Last High School Not CA and Student Is Under 19

- Contract with district + high school transcripts required.

### Out of Country School

- May use student visa in passport to prove they were abroad specifically to study. Cannot establish residency in another country on a student visa.

### Out of State School

- Use billing statement showing out-of-state tuition as one proof. Does **not** work for private colleges.
- For private or online schools: just need CA ID ≥ 1 year + 1 day and other proof.

## Student Records and Residency Evidence

### Out of State Taxes

- If student filed taxes out of state in past 2 years: need their most recent **CA 540**. Use CA ID ≥ 1 year + 1 day + CA 540.

### If Student Is Already CA Resident but Changing Citizenship Status

*(e.g., PR or DACA → US Citizen)*

- No new proof of residency needed. Just new citizenship documentation and a new application.
- US Citizenship Naturalization **supersedes all dates** — expired PR card doesn't matter once naturalized.

### Student Error

- Filed taxes outside CA for a recent year → must provide evidence they filed in CA.
- Put wrong country → fix it and make notes in ASPR.

### Two Years Care and Control Proof Flag

**If the student has no other residency flags**

- Update residency status back to R

**If the student has other residency flags**

!!! note
    "Two Years Care and Control Proof flagged students are fostered youth or wards of the court. They are **not** required to provide 2 proofs of residency. Instead, they can provide documentation showing they were under the care and control of a CA-resident adult for at least 2 years prior to the term start date."

- Student must provide documentation such as
    - Ward of the Court letters
    - Proof of Dependency cards
- From [Residency Overview p.25](https://www.cccco.edu/-/media/CCCCO-Website/docs/general/2025residencyoverviewdocument42125finala11y.pdf?la=en&hash=8A1227B6F9DB6D13138B0AF0E3E5EA4EF198232D)
    - "...districts will need to rely on
actual evidence and not a self-certification that the student is or was a dependent or ward
of the state through California's child welfare system (e.g., standardized documentation
currently given to foster youth by county welfare departments as evidence of this
transition for financial aid or other purposes, including “Ward of the Court” letters or
“Proof of Dependency” cards)."

### Unable to Establish CA Residency

- **TD/TN visa holders**: ineligible unless taking steps toward PR or out-of-status (expired visa).
- **Financially dependent on out-of-state/out-of-country parents** (more than $750/year).

## Unhoused Students

### Unhoused Youth

- Unhoused youth under 25 will be flagged as non-residents. Ask for usual documentation; note "student is unhoused youth under 25" in ASPR. Leave permanent address blank.
- Unhoused students: ask for CA ID or license + one form of documentation.
- See if they qualify for AB 540 as well.
- Refer to **Robert Manson**: (714) 564-6247.

### Unhoused Students

- Accept a **letter from their shelter** as one proof of residency.

## Other Scenarios

### Voted Outside of CA

- Does not need voter registration to disprove.
- Ask for regular 2 residency documents and update (per Pilar).

## General Additional Notes

- If student mentions B visa: need proof they are out of status (B visa = vacation visa, no school allowed).
- If we have the student's approval notice, the physical green card is **not required**.
- Port of entry documentation: if we can connect the dots they've been in CA and the date of establishing CA residency is within ~6 months of entry, they may have a case.
- If flagged for taxes or claimed by out-of-state parents: need **CA 540** as financial evidence.
- If approved for a previous semester but marked non-resident on a new application: can use previous semester's documents.
- **Always need a new application if changing residency or status for older semesters** (not required for the current semester).
- **Always need a reclass form unless it is the student's first semester with us.**
- Only need **one** reclass/AB 540/application if processing for multiple consecutive semesters — e.g., processing Fall 2019 also covers Spring 2020.
- If flagged as Non-Resident for an **SCC application**: send to SCC to fix it — we don't have access to their application.
- Students only need to provide documentation for the specific semester — just update on ASPR.

    !!! note "Regarding Financial Dependence from Natalie Wagner"
        Under Title 5, section 54032, financial independence weighs in favor of California residence, while financial dependence weighs against a finding of California residence. However, the regulation also provides some flexibility. Specifically, financial dependence may be overcome if the parent on whom the student is dependent is a California resident, or if there is no evidence that the student is maintaining residence in another state.

        In this situation, I would first consider whether the parent or individual claiming the student as a dependent is a California resident. If so, the student's financial dependence would generally not prevent reclassification, assuming the student has otherwise met the physical presence and intent requirements.

        Even if the parent resides outside California, Title 5, section 54032(d) allows districts to disregard a finding of financial dependence if there is no evidence that the student intends to establish or maintain residence elsewhere.

        Based on the information you provided, I would not necessarily require additional documentation from the student's parents solely because the student indicated that they are being claimed as a dependent. If the district is satisfied that the student has met the physical presence and intent requirements and there is no other evidence suggesting the student is maintaining residence outside California, I think the district could determine that the student meets the requirements for reclassification.
